## The thesis

Intuitive Machines is still priced as the company that couldn't land — two lunar landers tipped over on the Moon, and the stock never recovered from the punchline. But since January it has quietly assembled all three pieces of a missile-tracking satellite chokepoint: it bought Maxar's satellite bus manufacturing business (now Lanteris), it bought the Goonhilly and COMSAT deep-space ground station network, and the day after that ground deal closed, L3Harris awarded it an 18-spacecraft contract for the Space Development Agency's missile-tracking layer. No other commercial company holds all three links — build the spacecraft, fly it, and hear it come back — at once.

## Key numbers

- **Order backlog:** crossed $1.06B for the first time, and the company posted its first positive quarter on an adjusted EBITDA basis
- **Lanteris acquisition:** $800M, closed January 2026 — bought Maxar Space Systems' satellite manufacturing heritage outright
- **Backlog growth from acquisition:** 73% of the increase in backlog came from buying Lanteris, not from organically won work
- **Balance sheet strain:** cash fell more than 60% in a single quarter, free cash flow is negative, and a $500M ATM facility is live to sell more shares (217M shares outstanding, ~245M fully diluted)
- **Andromeda IDIQ:** a Space Force space-domain-awareness framework with a $6.24B ceiling — shared across 14 companies, with no task orders guaranteed

## The setup

The stock is down 65% from its 52-week high and the market is still trading the photograph of two landers on their sides. Underneath that, a missile-defense prime signed a contract for the bus — 18 IM-300 spacecraft platforms for AMDT3, awarded one day after the Goonhilly/COMSAT ground station deal closed — NASA kept adding task orders, and the Andromeda framework kept the door open. None of it is paying for a Moon landing. Q2 earnings land 13 August 2026, with more than a quarter of the free float sold short heading in.

## Risk factors

- Cash down over 60% in a single quarter, negative free cash flow, and continuing dilution via an active $500M ATM
- 73% of the backlog increase came from the Lanteris acquisition rather than organic wins — the AMDT3 contract itself rests on heritage and an L3Harris relationship Intuitive Machines bought in January, not capability it built from scratch
- Andromeda's $6.24B ceiling is shared with 13 other companies with zero task orders guaranteed
- Heavy short interest — more than a quarter of the float — heading into the 13 August earnings print

## What to watch

Q2 earnings on 13 August 2026 is the nearest catalyst, with the market watching whether manufacturing execution is starting to show up in the margins. Beyond that: the IM-3 "Trinity" lander headed to the magnetically anomalous Reiner Gamma swirl, and Altus-1, the first of a planned five-satellite lunar relay network. The gap between what retail is trading (the landings) and what the primes are signing (the bus) closes one of two ways — manufacturing shows up in the numbers, or the dilution outruns the wins.
