## What happened

Humana (HUM) has been in a sustained downtrend, driven by ongoing pressure across the healthcare insurance sector — particularly around rising medical costs and uncertainty in Medicare Advantage reimbursement rates.

These factors have weighed on profitability expectations and sentiment, contributing to the broader selloff seen across insurers.

As price now approaches the $149 level, the chart is testing a key area of historical support.

At the same time, MACD is beginning to show bullish divergence — with momentum starting to improve despite price pushing lower.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $149  
- Major downside support: ~$90  
- Resistance: Prior breakdown levels above current price  
- Momentum: MACD divergence forming  

## What would confirm the idea

A strong reaction at the $149 support level would be an early signal that buyers are stepping back in.

For confirmation, you would typically want to see:
- Price holding above support  
- MACD continuing to strengthen  
- A shift in short-term structure (higher lows forming)  

If this happens, it could indicate that this area is being used as an entry zone by market participants.

## What would weaken the idea

Failure to hold the $149 support level would invalidate the near-term bounce thesis.

A clean breakdown below support would suggest that sellers remain in control, opening the door for a move toward the next major support zone around $90.

In that scenario, the current divergence would likely fail.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
Price holds the $149 level, momentum continues to improve, and the stock begins forming a base. This could lead to a short-term bounce or trend stabilisation.

**Bearish scenario:**  
Support breaks, and price continues lower. The next meaningful area of interest sits significantly lower, near $90.

## Bottom line

Humana is approaching a critical technical level, with bullish MACD divergence suggesting that downside momentum may be fading.

This creates a potential bounce setup — but it is highly dependent on how price reacts at the $149 support level. Holding this zone could attract buyers, while a breakdown would shift focus much lower.
