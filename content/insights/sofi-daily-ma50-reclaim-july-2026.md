## What happened

SOFI has clawed back from a 52-week low near $14.92-$15.61 hit in May, reclaiming its 20-, 50-, and 100-day moving averages and now consolidating between roughly $17.50 and $18.50. Shares were up about 2.9% on Thursday to $18.25 on above-average volume, with the daily chart printing a series of higher lows since the spring bottom.

## Why it matters

SoFi is a digital consumer-finance platform spanning lending, banking, and investing, and this bounce is being driven by real, dated catalysts rather than broad market drift. In early July the company launched SoFi Small Business Loans (fixed loans up to $250,000, zero fees, funding within 24 hours) and stepped into the spotlight as a corporate backer of the federal Trump Accounts program, which the Treasury says has already enrolled nearly six million children with $1,000 federal contributions each — one analyst estimated it could pull in $100 billion of additional private contributions over the next year. That combination of a new revenue line and a high-visibility distribution partnership is what is pulling buyers back in, not a shift in the macro tape. For traders scanning for names with fresh bullish momentum signals, this is the kind of setup that shows up on a [top stocks with buy signals](/top-stocks-with-buy-signals) screen right now.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $17.50, with a broader floor near $16.00
- Resistance: $18.50, then $19.00
- Moving averages: price is holding above the 20-, 50-, and 100-day SMAs; the 200-day MA (~$23) remains well overhead
- Risk point: a daily close back below $16.00 would erase the reclaim and reopen the path to the May low

## What would confirm the idea

A push through $18.50-$19.00 on rising volume, continued higher lows holding the $17.50 shelf, and MACD staying constructively above its signal line would all support the reclaim carrying into the July 29 earnings date.

## What would weaken the idea

A failure to hold the mid-$17s, a MACD rollover back under its signal line, or broad risk-off pressure on high-beta fintech names ahead of earnings would all point to the bounce running out of steam before the print.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
SOFI clears $18.50-$19 resistance as the small-business-lending and Trump Accounts catalysts keep drawing buyers into the July 29 report, opening a path back toward the mid-$20s where the 200-day average sits.

**Bearish scenario:**  
The rally stalls at resistance, the mid-$17s support fails, and SOFI slides back toward the $16 floor as traders trim risk into earnings.

## Bottom line

SOFI's reclaim of its short-term moving averages on a genuine product and partnership catalyst is constructive, but the stock is still well below its 200-day average — this reads as a support-defense trade into earnings, not confirmation of a full trend reversal yet.
