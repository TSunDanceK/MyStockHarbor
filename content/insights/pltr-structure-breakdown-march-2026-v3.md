Palantir (PLTR) is beginning to show signs of weakness after a local break of structure on the daily timeframe.

This suggests the current move may be shifting into a pullback phase rather than continuation higher.

## Current Structure

Price has broken structure to the downside on the daily chart, which is often an early signal that momentum is weakening.

In addition:

- Price has moved below the weekly MA50  
- Price is now trending toward the weekly MA200  

This combination increases the probability of a deeper pullback.

## Key Levels to Watch

There are two major zones traders should be focused on:

### $90 Confluence Area

There is strong confluence around the $90 level, where:

- Previous price reactions have occurred  
- Moving averages are aligning  
- Structure may provide support  

This is the first area where buyers may step in.

### $66 Support (April Lows)

If price continues lower:

- A move toward the April support around $66 becomes likely  
- This level may act as a liquidity sweep zone  
- A reaction or bounce could occur after testing this level  

## Breakdown Scenario

The most important level to watch is:

### $60 Area

If PLTR closes below this zone:

- It would suggest a confirmed shift in trend  
- The structure would favour continued downside  
- The bullish structure would be invalidated  

## Lower Levels to Watch

In a confirmed breakdown scenario, the next key zones to watch for potential reactions would be:

- $40 area  
- $25 area  

These would likely act as longer-term support zones where buyers may re-enter.

## What This Means

PLTR is currently at a critical point:

- Hold above confluence → potential bounce  
- Sweep lower levels → possible recovery setup  
- Break below $60 → likely trend continuation lower  

How price behaves around these levels will define the next phase of the move.
