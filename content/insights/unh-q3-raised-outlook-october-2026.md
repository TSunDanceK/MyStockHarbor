## What happened

UnitedHealth [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000731766/000073176626000255/earningsrelease3q26unh.htm) on 13 October: adjusted earnings of $4.69 a share on revenue of $112.7bn, a little below the $113.2bn of the same quarter a year earlier. [Reuters reported](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/unitedhealth-group-raises-profit-forecast-100026393.html) that it lifted its 2026 adjusted earnings outlook to at least $20.50 a share, up from a prior range of $19.50 to $20, and the release credits a lower medical care ratio (88.4%, against 89.9% a year earlier) to medical cost management, pricing discipline and benefit design changes. That ratio included $610M of net favorable prior-period reserve development, which [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/13/unitedhealth-rises-4-on-earnings-beat-and-raised-full-year-outlook-humana-advances-2/) notes may not repeat. The same report shows medical membership down to 48.3 million from 50.1 million, and the release says Optum Health revenue fell 10% as it served fewer value-based care patients.
