## What happened

Unusual Machines, which makes NDAA-compliant drone parts such as motors, cameras and FPV goggles, [reported second-quarter 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1956955/000168316826006015/umac_ex9901.htm) on 6 August: revenue of $16.7M, up 687% on a year earlier, and a net loss of $7.8M. The same letter said third-quarter revenue was not expected to follow the earlier quarterly pattern and that gross margin was expected to fall from 34.7%, and on the [earnings call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/unusual-machines-umac-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) the CEO gave internal revenue aims of $12M to $14M for Q3 and $25M for Q4, which he said were not guidance. He also said more than 60,000 drones were expected to be ordered through the Pentagon's Drone Dominance program in the second half, mostly in Q4, and a [13 August proclamation](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/14/unusual-machines-soars-22-red-cat-climbs-8-ondas-gains-4-on-trumps-100-drone-tariff/) set new tariffs on imported drones and parts. The letter added that the largest customer was about 42% of Q2 revenue and that the Upgrade Energy battery acquisition was expected to close by the end of the third quarter.
