## What happened

Palantir [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/PLTR/palantir-reports-q3-2026-u-s-comm-revenue-growth-of-148-y-y-and-quyw16dj59bm.html) on 2 November, with revenue up 96% from a year earlier and a raised full-year revenue outlook. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/02/palantir-q3-results-stock-ai-trade-selloff/) reported that the results came after a sharp tech selloff, and that shares rose about 1.5% in after-hours trading right after the release. [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/palantir-stock-falls-over-3-despite-strong-q3-earnings-what-spooked-investors) reported a drop of about 3% in after-hours trading on 2 November and pointed to slower growth in total contract value and a high valuation. [FXEmpire](https://www.fxempire.com/forecasts/article/palantir-pltr-price-forecast-earnings-loom-as-downside-risks-build-1641701) reported on 30 October, before the results, that the shares had closed below their 50-day average.
