## What happened

Chewy has gone from a steady 2025 uptrend to a hard multi-month slide, falling from a 52-week high near $48.62 to the low $20s. That decline has now stalled. Over the past several weeks price has stopped printing new lows and is instead oscillating in a mid-channel range, with the Bollinger bands starting to pinch together as volatility compresses. This is not a stock in a clean uptrend and it is not a single-day spike — it is a name that fell hard, found a shelf of buyers, and is now chopping sideways while it works out what it wants to do next.

## Why it matters

Chewy sells pet food, supplies, and pharmacy products online, with a subscription-heavy Autoship base that now accounts for the large majority of sales. The decline into this base was driven by a real mix of pressures: a weaker consumer spending backdrop that forced management to trim its sales outlook, a California antitrust inquiry, insider selling around the CEO's conference appearances, and the market digesting integration risk from the Modern Animal acquisition. None of that is invented — it is the documented sequence behind the fall from the highs.

What has changed more recently is retail positioning. Reddit sentiment on the name has swung sharply bullish, with traders building an accumulation case around Chewy's low-debt balance sheet, consistent cash generation, and a speculative take-private/M&A narrative. That is classic behavior for a beaten-down retail favorite entering a basing phase — the stock stops falling, chatter builds around "this is too cheap," and positioning shifts from capitulation to accumulation before the market has actually confirmed a new trend. ## Levels to watch

- Support: $20.40-$20.90 (recent range floor)
- Resistance: $23.70-$24.00 (range ceiling / basing ceiling)
- Moving averages: Price remains below its longer-term daily averages, consistent with a post-decline base rather than an established uptrend
- Risk point: A daily close below $20.40 on rising volume would break the base and reopen the path toward the 52-week low

## What would confirm the idea

A genuine base needs to keep tightening on shrinking volume, with the Bollinger bands continuing to narrow rather than expanding on down days. Confirmation would come from a series of higher lows inside the range, a volume pickup on up-days versus down-days, and eventually a push through the $23.70-$24.00 ceiling with expansion in the bands — the classic signature of accumulation resolving into a new leg higher.

## What would weaken the idea

If price breaks $20.40 with volume expanding on the downside, that is not a pullback within the base, it is the base failing. A weak or in-line earnings print that reignites the sales-forecast concerns, or a further deterioration in consumer spending commentary, would also undercut the retail accumulation thesis and put the 52-week low back in play.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
The base continues to tighten, retail accumulation persists, and Chewy holds the $20.40 floor into its next earnings report. A stabilizing or better-than-feared print lets the stock clear $24 and begin repairing the technical damage from the multi-month decline.

**Bearish scenario:**  
Consumer spending pressure shows up again in the numbers, the acquisition-thesis chatter fades, and sellers re-take control below $20.40. The base fails and CHWY revisits its 52-week low as valuation concerns (still elevated relative to peers) resurface.

## Bottom line

CHWY is a name retail traders are actively debating right now, and the chart backs up a genuine basing setup rather than a trend or a spike — the stock has stopped falling and is compressing in a tightening range after a brutal drawdown. The next earnings report is likely to decide whether this becomes a real accumulation base or just a pause before more downside.
