## What happened

After topping out near $28 in June, GME pulled back and settled into a tightening range roughly between $21 and $23. Volume has faded from spikes above 5 million shares to consistently under 3 million, a classic footprint of sellers drying up. Price has tested the $20.93 area more than once without a decisive breakdown, while resistance has capped near $23.11.

## Why it matters

GME remains one of the most closely watched retail tickers, and it's still in the headlines for reasons that have nothing to do with a clean technical breakout. Ryan Cohen's renewed pursuit of eBay, including a proposed $125-per-share acquisition and shareholder approval to expand GameStop's share count to help fund it, is keeping the name in constant chatter. A new nationwide on-demand delivery partnership with Uber Eats adds another data point in the company's push to diversify beyond legacy retail. None of that has produced a trend change yet — this is a name still building a floor, not confirming a breakout. Base-building phases like this typically resolve once the corporate-action headlines settle and positioning clears out. ## Levels to watch

- Support: $20.93 (late-June low, first line of defense)
- Resistance: $23.11 (early-July high, base ceiling)
- Moving averages: 20-day Bollinger midline near $22 acting as an intraday pivot
- Risk point: A daily close below $20.93 that doesn't quickly reclaim it would break the base and open room toward the 52-week low near $19.93

## What would confirm the idea

A volume-backed push through $23.11 with the Bollinger bands expanding outward (rather than the current squeeze) would confirm buyers are stepping back in and the base is resolving higher.

## What would weaken the idea

A move back below $20.93 on rising volume, especially if it coincides with a setback in the eBay pursuit or a stall in the Uber Eats rollout, would signal the range is failing rather than basing.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
GME holds the $21 floor, the Bollinger squeeze resolves upward, and a confirmed breakout above $23.11 opens a path back toward the low-to-mid $20s and eventually the 52-week high near $28.

**Bearish scenario:**  
Support gives way on heavier volume, the eBay pursuit loses steam or draws a cool reception from shareholders or regulators, and price slips toward the $19.93 52-week low, extending the range lower.

## Bottom line

GME hasn't broken out yet — it's quietly building a base above support while the eBay and Uber Eats headlines keep retail attention locked on the name. The next real signal comes from which side of $21-$23 gives way first.
