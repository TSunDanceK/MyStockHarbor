## What happened

Strategy (MSTR) has staged a sharp V-shaped reversal after setting a fresh 23-month low near $82 on June 26. The stock has rallied back toward $100-104, gaining as much as 10% intraday today, after the company unveiled a new capital-management framework designed to reduce balance-sheet stress.

This follows directly from the weekly MA200 breakdown flagged in the prior update: the stock fell further after that break, bottomed near $82, and is now attempting its first real recovery bounce.

## Why it matters

Strategy's stock has been trading like a leveraged Bitcoin vehicle with a software business attached, and the last several weeks show exactly that dynamic in both directions — a hard slide into the low-$80s as Bitcoin weakened, followed by a fast bounce on a company-specific catalyst.

The catalyst here is real and structural: a Digital Credit Capital Framework that formalizes a USD cash reserve, authorizes up to $1 billion in MSTR buybacks and a separate $1 billion for its preferred securities, and creates a controlled Bitcoin monetization program to fund obligations without relying purely on new equity issuance. That matters because it directly addresses the mNAV-inversion risk flagged in the prior update — giving the company tools to manage dilution and preferred dividend costs even while mNAV stays under pressure.

This is a company-specific announcement, not a Bitcoin-driven move, which is why it's worth separating from the broader crypto tape. ## Levels to watch

- Support: $82, the June 26 low that anchors the recent range
- Resistance: The mid-$120s-130s zone, the level the stock slid from before this selloff
- Moving averages: Daily MACD is the trend/momentum reference for this bounce, given the fast reversal
- Risk point: A slide back below $95-97 would suggest today's bounce is fading rather than building a base

## What would confirm the idea

A close above $104 followed by consolidation in the $100-110 range over several sessions, without a fast round-trip back toward $82, would confirm buyers are treating the new capital framework as a genuine de-risking event rather than a one-day news pop.

## What would weaken the idea

A fast reversal back below $95-97, especially if Bitcoin resumes weakening toward $60,000, would suggest the bounce was a short-covering rally rather than a durable shift, and would put the $82 low back in play.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
MSTR holds above $100, Bitcoin stabilizes, and the new capital framework is rewarded with a sustained re-rating as the market prices in reduced dilution and preferred-dividend risk.

**Bearish scenario:**  
MSTR fails to hold the bounce, Bitcoin resumes its slide, and the stock retests or breaks the $82 low as the structural mNAV pressure outweighs the new capital-management tools.

## Bottom line

MSTR has shown its first real recovery bounce since the weekly MA200 break, built on a genuine company-specific catalyst rather than just a Bitcoin rally — holding above $100 is the next test for whether this becomes a real base or just a relief bounce.
