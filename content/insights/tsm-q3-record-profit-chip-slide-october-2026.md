## What happened

TSMC [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-10/526b65af7305df2dde6b9ece6812b1fdf1e49f9b/3Q26%20EarningsRelease.pdf) on 15 October: revenue of US$46.72bn, net income of NT$763.06bn (up 68.7% from a year earlier) and a gross margin of 66.4%. It guided fourth-quarter revenue to US$52.3bn to US$54.3bn. [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/15/tsmc-just-posted-record-profit-for-the-sixth-straight-quarter-why-did-the-stock-fall/) reported that the US-listed shares fell on the day despite the results, tying the move to a wider pullback in AI stocks; it separately quoted chief executive C.C. Wei saying consumer and price-sensitive end markets are being challenged. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-chip-stocks-set-to-wipe-out-2-trillion-after-latest-selloff-4952088) reported on 15 October that US chip stocks were set to lose about $2 trillion in market value, extending a selloff over the prior six sessions, citing rate-hike bets and concerns over the cost of the AI buildout. [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/TW_2330.TW_2026-10-15) summarised the earnings call as saying gross margin eased from the previous quarter mainly because of the early ramp of 2nm production, and that overseas fabs may dilute margins more in coming years.
