## What happened

SOUN has fallen roughly 71% from its 52-week high of $22.17 to the mid-$6 range, but the decline has stalled. Over recent weeks price has compressed into a tight $6.30-$6.90 band, with small daily ranges and muted volume replacing the sharp downtrend that defined the first half of the year. That's the signature of a base forming, not a stock still in freefall.

## Why it matters

SoundHound is a name retail traders know well — it was previously driven to a 52-week high by meme-stock buying, and it continues to generate heavy chatter on Stocktwits and Reddit. Two recent AI industry awards, a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader placement and an "Agentic AI Company of the Year" honor, gave the stock a chance to run on good news. Instead, shares merely steadied. That muted reaction to genuinely positive headlines is informative: buyers are absorbing available supply without urgency, which is more consistent with quiet accumulation than with a name about to break out on hype. This move is not being driven by news or earnings — it's a technical standoff between buyers and sellers within a well-defined range. ## Levels to watch

- Support: $6.30
- Resistance: $6.90
- Moving averages: price remains well below its longer-term moving averages given the scale of the prior decline from $22
- Risk point: a daily close back below the $5.83 52-week low

## What would confirm the idea

A decisive breakout above $6.90 resistance on above-average volume, ideally paired with a pickup in Stocktwits mention volume, would suggest accumulation is turning into active demand rather than passive base-building.

## What would weaken the idea

A break below $6.30, especially on rising volume, would suggest the base is failing and sellers remain in control. A retest and failure to hold the $5.83 52-week low would invalidate the accumulation thesis entirely.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
SOUN holds the $6.30 floor, absorbs the remaining overhead supply, and eventually clears $6.90 as the agentic AI award narrative draws fresh retail buying, opening room back toward prior swing highs in the $8-$10 area.

**Bearish scenario:**  
The range breaks to the downside, the stock revisits and possibly undercuts its $5.83 52-week low, and the broader downtrend from $22 resumes as persistent losses and slowing momentum outweigh the AI narrative.

## Bottom line

SOUN's steep decline has given way to a tight, low-drama base rather than a fresh trend — the setup favors patience over chasing until price actually clears $6.90.
