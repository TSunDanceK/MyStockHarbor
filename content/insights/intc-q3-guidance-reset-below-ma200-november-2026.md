## What happened

Intel [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1786/intel-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results) on 29 October: revenue of $16.5bn, up 21% from a year earlier, with data centre and AI revenue up 78%. It guided fourth-quarter revenue to $15bn to $16bn, and its chief executive said the immediate priority is to unlock more supply for its Xeon server chips. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/intel-signals-slower-growth-as-pc-market-remains-under-pressure-4984967) reported that the outlook fell short of the analyst average of about $17.07bn and pointed to supply constraints, an October explosion at its plant in Kiryat Gat, Israel, and weaker PC demand linked to higher memory prices. [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/price-intel-nasdaq-intc-stock-crosses-below-200-day-moving-average-following-analyst-downgrade-2026-10-30/) reported that the shares traded below their 200-day average on 30 October, and that Seaport Research Partners downgraded the stock to neutral while JPMorgan and Susquehanna lowered their price estimates.
