## What happened

RIOT ran to a 52-week-adjacent high near $27-30 in late June, then reversed sharply, bottoming out around $18-19 in mid-July. Since then it has stopped trending and started compressing - daily ranges have narrowed and the stock has printed a series of higher lows inside a rough $18-25 band. That's the footprint of a base being built after a decline, not a stock still in a clean uptrend and not a single-day spike.

## Why it matters

RIOT is one of the most actively discussed retail names in the bitcoin-mining group, and the story behind the current chart is more interesting than the price action alone. Bitcoin itself has been soft, down toward the low-$60,000s recently, which would normally drag miner stocks lower with it. Instead, RIOT has held its recent low and analysts have gone the other way: Citi lifted its target from $21 to $28 and BTIG went from $28 to $40, both citing RIOT's pivot toward AI and data-center power capacity rather than pure bitcoin exposure. That divergence - equity holding up while the underlying commodity slips - is a sentiment and positioning signal, not a news-driven spike; there's no single headline driving this, it's traders and funds repricing the AI-infrastructure optionality while bitcoin chops. For readers tracking names that have pulled back meaningfully from their highs and are working through a base, this setup fits the pattern covered in our [stocks down 20% from all-time highs](/stocks-down-20-from-all-time-highs) screen.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $18-19 (the mid-July low and the base's lower boundary)
- Resistance: $25-27 (upper edge of the current range, below the June high)
- Moving averages: Short-term averages have flattened as the range has tightened, with longer-term support still well beneath current price
- Risk point: A daily close below $18 on rising volume, which would break the pattern of higher lows

## What would confirm the idea

A push through $25-27 on expanding volume would signal the base is resolving higher, especially if it happens alongside stabilizing bitcoin prices. Continued higher lows within the range - even without a breakout yet - are also a healthy sign that demand is stepping in on dips rather than sellers overwhelming the tape.

## What would weaken the idea

A daily close back below $18 would undo the higher-lows structure and open the door to a retest of deeper support. A renewed leg down in bitcoin that drags the whole miner group lower, or a Q2 report on July 30 that undercuts the AI-infrastructure narrative analysts have been pricing in, would both weaken the case.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
RIOT holds the $18-19 floor into earnings, clears $25-27 on strong volume, and the AI/data-center narrative gets reinforced by the Q2 report - opening a path back toward the $27-30 area.

**Bearish scenario:**  
Bitcoin extends its slide, RIOT breaks $18 support, and a soft Q2 print on July 30 removes the analyst-upgrade tailwind, sending the stock back toward its deeper 52-week lows.

## Bottom line

RIOT is basing, not breaking out - the trade isn't in the range itself but in how it resolves. With earnings landing July 30, the base is likely to get tested almost immediately; a hold above $18 with a move through $25-27 is the bullish trigger, not the consolidation alone.
