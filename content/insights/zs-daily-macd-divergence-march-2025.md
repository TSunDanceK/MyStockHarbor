## 🧠 Company overview  
Zscaler Inc. is a leading cloud cybersecurity company, specialising in zero-trust network access and secure cloud infrastructure. Their platform enables businesses to protect users, data, and applications without relying on traditional network perimeters.

## 📰 Recent context  
Zscaler continues to operate in the high-growth cybersecurity space, particularly within zero-trust architecture. However, like many growth tech stocks, it has experienced pressure from broader market conditions, valuation compression, and shifting expectations around growth.

---

## 📉 What happened

ZS is now entering a strong support zone around the $140 level.

The stock has been trading within a large internal range since August 2020, roughly between $84 and $376. More recently, price action has turned bearish after failing to reach prior highs in October 2025 and breaking below the $165 structure level.

Despite this, over the past ~90 days we have seen clear bullish divergence forming on the MACD on the daily timeframe.

---

## 🧠 Why it matters

This divergence is significant.

Price has been making lower lows, while the MACD has been forming higher lows. This is a classic bullish divergence pattern, often interpreted as a sign of weakening selling pressure and potential accumulation at lower levels.

Given the broader range structure, this area becomes particularly interesting as it sits within the lower portion of a long-term range — a zone where buyers have historically stepped in.

---

## 🎯 Levels to watch

**Support:**  
- $140 (current key support zone)  
- $120 (volume area low / deeper support)

**Resistance:**  
- $165 (major structure level)

**Moving averages:**  
- Not the primary focus in this range-driven setup

**Risk point:**  
- Sustained acceptance below $120

---

## ✅ What would confirm the idea

- Strong reaction and bounce from the $140 zone  
- Continued strength or crossover on the MACD  
- Daily closes back above $165  

A move back above $165 would suggest a reclaim of structure and increase the probability of a move back into the range.

---

## ❌ What would weaken the idea

- Clean breakdown below $140  
- Loss of MACD divergence structure  
- Continued price acceptance below $120  

This would suggest further downside and potential breakdown of the broader range.

---

## ⚖️ Bull vs Bear scenarios

**Bull case:**  
Support holds at $140, divergence plays out, and price reclaims $165 — opening the door for a move higher within the range.

**Bear case:**  
Support fails, price moves toward the $120 liquidity zone, and the range begins to break down.

---

## 🧾 Bottom line

Zscaler is currently sitting at a key decision point.

You have a combination of major support, long-term range positioning, and bullish MACD divergence. However, the structure remains bearish unless price can reclaim the $165 level.

This is a high-interest zone rather than a confirmed reversal, and traders will be watching closely for either a strong reaction from support or continuation lower toward $120.
