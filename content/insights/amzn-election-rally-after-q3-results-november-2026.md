## What happened

Amazon [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-earnings-q3-2026-report) on 29 October, with AWS sales of $46.0 billion, up 39% from a year earlier, and a 2026 capital spending outlook of about $220 billion. On 4 November, [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/04/amazon-microsoft-and-other-tech-giants-close-higher-after-democrats-midterm-sweep/) reported that the shares closed about 6% higher after the midterm results, and that some analysts tied the rise to the idea that data center restrictions could favour companies that already have large networks. [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/04/stock-market-today-nov-4-amazon-jumps-6-from-new-perspective-on-aws-growth-and-ai-strategy-following-election-results/) said investors were reassessing the earnings.
