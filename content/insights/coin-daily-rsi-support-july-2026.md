## What happened

Coinbase (COIN) has bounced sharply off the $140-160 area, the same major weekly support zone flagged in the prior setup, after the stock fell further than expected following a weak Q1 earnings reaction. Shares jumped 11.3% on the initial Open USD stablecoin partnership news, then added another 4.1% the following session, pushing the stock back toward the high-$160s.

This is the clearest test yet of whether the $140-160 zone can hold as long-term support after months of pressure from softer crypto trading volumes and a difficult earnings backdrop.

## Why it matters

Coinbase's business is highly sensitive to crypto trading activity and sentiment, and the stock has been under sustained pressure, down close to 30% year-to-date even after this bounce. The Open USD news is a genuine structural catalyst rather than a speculative pop: partnering with Visa, Mastercard, BlackRock and Google on a shared-reserve stablecoin puts Coinbase at the center of a large new payments infrastructure push, directly competing with rivals like Circle's stablecoin, which fell sharply on the same news.

Coinbase also secured a MiCA license and opened a new EU hub in Luxembourg in late June, adding a regulatory expansion angle alongside the stablecoin story. Together, these are real, verifiable developments — not rumor-driven speculation — which is why the bounce off the $140-160 support zone looks more substantial than a typical dead-cat reaction.

This kind of support-zone bounce fits the pattern tracked on the [oversold stocks](/oversold-stocks-today) list, since COIN had been pushed to a deeply depressed level before this reversal.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $140-160, the major multi-year support zone the stock has now bounced from twice
- Resistance: $178-190, the area where prior bounce attempts have stalled
- Moving averages: Daily RSI(14) is the momentum reference for this bounce, given how sharply oversold conditions reversed
- Risk point: A slide back below $150 would suggest the Open USD bounce is fading and the support zone is at risk of failing

## What would confirm the idea

A close above $178-190 on continued strong volume, with follow-through data showing real adoption of Open USD among partner platforms, would confirm the bounce is building into a genuine trend change rather than a short-lived news reaction.

## What would weaken the idea

A fast round-trip back below $150 would suggest the rally was a one-off reaction to the stablecoin headline rather than a durable shift in sentiment, and would put the multi-year support zone at real risk of failing for the first time.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
COIN holds above $160, Open USD gains real adoption traction among its 140+ partner companies, and the stock reclaims $178-190 as the market re-rates Coinbase's role in stablecoin infrastructure.

**Bearish scenario:**  
COIN fails to hold the bounce, crypto trading volumes stay soft, and the stock slides back through the $140-160 zone, confirming the multi-year support has finally broken.

## Bottom line

Coinbase has bounced off a critical multi-year support zone on a genuine structural catalyst rather than pure sentiment — holding above $160 is the next test for whether this becomes a real trend change or just a relief rally.
