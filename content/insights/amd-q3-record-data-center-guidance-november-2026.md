## What happened

AMD [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amd-reports-third-quarter-2026-211500470.html) on 3 November, with revenue of $13.38 billion, up 45% from a year earlier, and data center revenue of $8.5 billion, up 97%. The company also guided fourth-quarter revenue to about $17 billion, plus or minus 3%. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/amd-stock-jumps-after-hours-as-quarterly-results-revenue-guidance-beat-estimates-4996344) reported that adjusted earnings and the fourth-quarter outlook were both above analyst consensus, and that the shares rose more than 7% after hours after falling 17.5% in October. Gaming revenue fell 40% in the quarter, according to the release. For the suppliers and customers behind the story, see the [AMD bottleneck page](/bottlenecks/amd).
