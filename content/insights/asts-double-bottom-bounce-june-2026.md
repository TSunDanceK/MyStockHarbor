## What happened

After printing an all-time high of $133.86 on May 28, ASTS collapsed nearly 51% in just four weeks — one of the sharpest corrections in the stock's history. The move down wasn't a slow grind; it was a near-vertical unwind of speculative positioning built up around the satellite launch narrative and SpaceX IPO momentum.

Price has now carved out a double bottom at approximately $63–$64, touching that level twice over consecutive sessions with buyers stepping in both times. Today the stock is bouncing back to the $71–$72 area. RSI reached extreme oversold territory during the flush — a reading that, for a stock this volatile, historically coincides with at least a short-term mean reversion move.

## Why it matters

ASTS is not a value stock — it's a high-conviction, high-risk satellite infrastructure play that trades almost entirely on execution milestones and sentiment around the direct-to-device (D2D) broadband thesis. When positioning unwinds in stocks like this, the moves are violent and fast, and that's exactly what happened after the May peak.

The correction was partly forced by the SpaceX IPO in mid-June, which caused capital rotation out of "SpaceX proxy" names — ASTS, RKLB, and LUNR all sold off sharply as real SpaceX equity became available. That's a positioning-driven move, not a fundamental deterioration. Operationally, the business is progressing: BlueBird 8, 9, and 10 launched successfully on June 17, FCC commercial authorization for the U.S. is in place, and the company holds $3.5B in cash with $1.2B+ in contracted revenue commitments.

For traders watching heavily discounted high-growth names, [stocks down 20% or more from all-time highs](/stocks-down-20-from-all-time-highs) that retain intact fundamental theses are often where the sharpest bounces originate — and ASTS fits that profile here.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $63–$64 (double bottom lows, twice-tested buyer zone)
- Resistance: $75–$76 (prior breakdown level, first meaningful overhead supply), then $90–$91
- Moving averages: MA50 and MA200 are both well above current price following the crash — these act as resistance targets on any sustained recovery, not support
- Risk point: A daily close below $62 with volume would break the double bottom structure and signal the flush is not done

## What would confirm the idea

A reclaim of $75 on above-average volume would be the first real confirmation that the double bottom is holding and that supply is being absorbed. A series of higher lows above $64, combined with RSI building back above 40 from oversold levels, would strengthen the case further. The next meaningful catalyst window is the Q2 earnings report on August 17 — a beat or reaffirmed guidance there could re-accelerate the recovery.

## What would weaken the idea

If the $63–$64 zone breaks on a daily close with heavy selling volume, the double bottom fails and the next meaningful support cluster is back around $50–$52. Continued insider selling (which has been notable in recent months) alongside broad market risk-off would also undermine any bounce attempt. If Q2 revenue again misses materially — particularly if the sequential build in H2 starts to look implausible — the re-rating lower could have further to run.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
The double bottom at $64 holds, buyers absorb supply as the SpaceX rotation trade fades, and ASTS grinds back toward $90+ through Q3 as BlueBird deployment milestones stack up. August earnings deliver the first clear sign of H2 revenue acceleration, re-engaging longer-term growth investors.

**Bearish scenario:**  
The bounce stalls at $75–$76 resistance, selling resumes, and the $63 support breaks. The narrative shifts from "buying the dip" to "catching a falling knife" as the market questions whether the $150–200M full-year guidance is achievable after two consecutive massive revenue misses. The stock grinds lower toward the $50s as speculative froth continues to wash out.

## Bottom line

ASTS is at a genuine decision point — a double bottom at $64 after a historically fast 51% correction, with RSI at oversold extremes and the fundamental thesis still intact. The setup has real bounce potential, but this is a stock that rewards patience over aggression: wait for the $75 reclaim before adding conviction, and keep the $62 close firmly in mind as the line where the thesis breaks.
