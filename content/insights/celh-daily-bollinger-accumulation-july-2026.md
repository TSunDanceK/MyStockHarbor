## What happened

Celsius has gone from a $66.74 high earlier this year to trading near $28, a roughly 58% drawdown, but the free-fall has stopped. Since bottoming near $27.55 in early June, price has compressed into a tightening range roughly between $28 and $31, with the 20-day Bollinger Band narrowing week over week — the hallmark of an accumulation base rather than continued distribution.

## Why it matters

Celsius is a stock retail traders never stopped talking about — Reddit and Stocktwits threads on CELH have stayed active through the entire decline, and the ticker still ranks among the platforms' most-discussed consumer names. The reason for the pullback is real and traceable: the UK's April 2027 ban on high-caffeine drink sales to under-16s and a Texas AG probe into marketing practices, layered on top of slowing core-brand growth and margin dilution from the Alani Nu and Rockstar acquisitions. This is not a random single-day drop — it's a multi-month grind lower that has now stalled, and several sell-side desks (Stifel, Citi, BofA, Morgan Stanley) cut targets while keeping Buy ratings, implying they see this as a valuation reset rather than a broken story. For context, see [stocks down 20% from all-time highs](/stocks-down-20-from-all-time-highs) for other names in a similar post-selloff basing phase.

## Levels to watch

- Support: $27.50–$27.86 (the June low, tested multiple times)
- Resistance: $30.64–$31.53 (top of the current range / 20-day MA)
- Moving averages: Price remains below both the 50-day and 200-day averages, confirming the longer-term trend is still down even as the short-term range tightens
- Risk point: A daily close back below $27.50 would break the base and open the door to a retest of deeper 2026 lows

## What would confirm the idea

A push through $31 on rising volume, especially heading into or after the August 6 earnings report, would confirm buyers are absorbing supply rather than just marking time. Narrowing Bollinger Bands followed by an expansion to the upside is the classic signature of a base resolving higher.

## What would weaken the idea

A breakdown below $27.50 on heavy volume — particularly if it comes with another negative earnings or regulatory headline — would signal the range was distribution, not accumulation, and the downtrend is resuming.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
CELH holds the $27.50 floor through earnings, the range keeps tightening, and a volume-backed breakout above $31 triggers a move back toward the $40-plus levels analysts still see as fair value.

**Bearish scenario:**  
Weak Q2 numbers or fresh regulatory noise breaks the $27.50 floor, the base fails, and CELH grinds toward new 52-week lows as the market prices in a longer margin-recovery timeline.

## Bottom line

Celsius looks like a stock building a floor rather than falling through one — the range has tightened for six weeks even as the news flow stayed negative, which is often how real bases form. The August 6 earnings print is the catalyst that decides whether this becomes a launchpad or another leg down.
