## What happened

ASML [reported third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000937966/000162828026065813/pressreleasefinancialresul.htm) on 14 October: what ASML called record net sales of EUR 11.7bn, a gross margin of 57.5% and net income of EUR 5.0bn. In the same release, ASML said that its planned supply for 2027 and 2028 is not expected to fully meet customer demand, and that it is looking at 2028 capacity beyond the 30% increase previously communicated. [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/asml-says-2027-and-2028-supply-will-fall-short-of-demand-as-q3-sales-hit-a-record/) noted that Amsterdam shares rose in the morning while the Nasdaq-listed shares were lower, and [Investing.com](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/asml-q3-2026-slides-record-results-but-shares-fall-on-valuation-93CH-4909050) linked the US reaction to valuation, saying much of the strength may already have been priced in. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/asml-raises-sales-forecast-third-061145434.html) instead pointed to third-quarter sales that narrowly missed one analyst estimate, so the sources differ on the reason.
