## What happened

META fell roughly 14.5% in the first half of 2026 and hit its 2026 lows earlier this year, but has since rebounded about 20% off those lows. The stock is now trading in the low $630s, within roughly 2% of its daily 200-day moving average near $642 — its first real test of that long-term trendline since the drawdown began.

## Why it matters

A mega-cap like Meta testing its 200-day MA after a genuine multi-week recovery is a different signal than a stock merely drifting near an average — it reflects real trend repair after a real drawdown. The setup is also catalyst-led: Meta just released Muse Spark 1.1, an AI model aimed squarely at coding and agentic tasks, positioning it as a direct challenger to OpenAI and Anthropic in one of the most closely watched corners of AI. Cathie Wood's ARK backed that narrative with a fresh $20.6 million share purchase, reversing an earlier period of profit-taking. Retail sentiment has moved with it too, shifting from "bullish" to "extremely bullish" on Stocktwits alongside a sharp jump in message volume. Together this is a technical, catalyst, and sentiment-led setup rather than a coincidence of proximity to a moving average. ## Levels to watch

- Support: $610-$615, the recent higher-low structure formed during the rebound
- Resistance: $642, the daily 200-day moving average and the May 28 swing high
- Moving averages: price is testing the daily 200-day MA from below for the first time since the H1 2026 decline
- Risk point: a rejection at $642 followed by a break back under $610

## What would confirm the idea

A decisive daily close above the 200-day MA near $642, ideally followed by a push toward the unfilled post-April-earnings gap around $688, would confirm buyers are back in control and the recovery has legs.

## What would weaken the idea

Rejection at the 200-day MA followed by a slide back below the recent higher-low support near $610 would suggest the rebound was a bounce within a larger downtrend rather than genuine trend repair.

## Bull vs bear scenarios

**Bullish scenario:**  
META clears $642 on conviction, the Muse Spark narrative gains commercial traction, and shares grind toward the $688 gap-fill level as institutional buying continues.

**Bearish scenario:**  
The 200-day MA holds as resistance, momentum stalls after the recent run, and META drifts back toward the low-$600s as investors wait for the next earnings report to confirm AI-driven growth.

## Bottom line

META is running its first genuine test of the 200-day MA since this year's drawdown, with a real product catalyst and fresh institutional buying behind it — a clean break above $642 would mark a meaningful trend-repair signal, not just proximity to an average.
