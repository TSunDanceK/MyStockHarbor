## What happened

Shopify reported third-quarter 2026 results on 3 November, with revenue of $3.74bn, up 32% from a year earlier. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/shopify-stock-tanks-as-q3-revenue-misses-estimates-for-first-time-since-2022-133246370.html) reported that revenue and gross merchandise volume were just below analyst estimates, and that the shares fell in premarket trading on 3 November. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-shopify-tops-profit-view-in-q3-2026-but-shares-fall-93CH-4995839) said the fourth-quarter outlook includes revenue growth in the low-30% range, and that investors focused on the outlook, planned spending and high expectations. [The Deep Dive](https://thedeepdive.ca/shopify-sees-net-profit-jump-75-in-q3-2026-but-shares-sink-on-revenue-miss/) reported free cash flow of $816m and a 75% rise in net income.
