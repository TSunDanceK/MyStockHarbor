  AbbVie reported $17.4 billion of net revenues in the third quarter of 2026,
  about $13.3 billion in the United States and $4.1 billion abroad. Its 10-K
  says Skyrizi and Rinvoq together made up approximately 42% of 2025 net
  revenues. On the customer side, the 10-K names three wholesale distributors,
  McKesson, Cardinal Health and Cencora, as accounting for substantially all
  of its US pharmaceutical product sales, with none above 43% of 2025 US gross
  revenues. On the supply side, the 10-K says AbbVie buys certain products and
  services from a limited number of suppliers and, in some cases, a single
  supply source, but names none of them. The named supply-side rows are
  therefore partners behind specific medicines: Janssen for Imbruvica, Gedeon
  Richter for Vraylar and Genmab for Epkinly.
