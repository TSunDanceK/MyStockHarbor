summary: >
  ExxonMobil produces oil and gas, refines it and sells fuels, chemicals and
  specialty products. Its listed parent has been ExxonMobil Holdings
  Corporation, still trading as XOM, since a 1 July 2026 reorganization that
  moved the company's home to Texas. It produced about 4.7 million oil-equivalent
  barrels a day in 2025. Its 10-K does cite exposure to third-party rigs,
  shipping and service providers, and its biggest projects run through named
  partners and contractors: Chevron and CNOOC in Guyana, QatarEnergy at
  Golden Pass, and rig, subsea and floating-vessel contractors in Guyana. The
  parts of its filings reviewed name no individual customer.
