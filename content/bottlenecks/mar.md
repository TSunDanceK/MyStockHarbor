summary: >
  Marriott is an asset-light franchisor and manager rather than a hotel
  owner - it owns well under 1% of the rooms flying its flags, so its real
  operational dependencies are the property owners, distribution channels,
  and technology vendors that keep bookings and properties running. On the
  revenue side, Marriott doesn't have customer concentration in the usual
  sense (tens of millions of individual travelers); the concentrated
  relationships that actually exist are with co-brand card issuers and
  online travel agencies that fund and channel a meaningful share of its
  economics.

customersNote: >

  Bonvoy loyalty-program economics, online travel agencies that channel a
  large share of paid booking volume, and Marriott Vacations Worldwide,
  which licenses Marriott's brand names for a fee.
