summary: >
  ADP is a payroll, HR, benefits administration and PEO (co-employment)
  services company that served over 1.1 million clients and generated
  about $20.6 billion in revenue in fiscal 2025. Because ADP sells to
  employers of virtually every size and industry, its real bottlenecks
  sit on the supply side: the cloud infrastructure, payment rails and
  insurance-carrier network it needs to actually move money and benefits
  on behalf of clients. On the revenue side, ADP is intentionally
  diversified rather than customer-concentrated, so the "customers"
  chart below reflects where its revenue is genuinely concentrated by
  business line and geography rather than any single client.
customersNote: >
  ADP's 10-K states that no single client or group of affiliated clients

  chart breaks down ADP's roughly $20.6 billion in fiscal 2025 revenue
  by client segment and geography (Employer Services vs. PEO Services,
  and domestic vs. international within Employer Services) to show
  where revenue is actually concentrated.
