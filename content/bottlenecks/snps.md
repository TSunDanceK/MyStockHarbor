summary: >
  Synopsys makes the electronic design automation (EDA) software chipmakers
  use to design semiconductors, so it has no physical parts supply chain -
  instead its tools have to stay certified against the world's leading chip
  foundries' manufacturing processes, and its cloud-based EDA workloads
  depend on major hyperscalers for compute. On the revenue side, Synopsys's
  own 10-K discloses dependence on a relatively small number of large
  customers, with independent reporting pointing to a major foundry
  customer's pullback hurting fiscal 2025 results.
