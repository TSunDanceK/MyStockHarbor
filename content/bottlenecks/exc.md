summary: >
  Exelon has been a pure-play, regulated transmission-and-distribution
  utility holding company since spinning off its generation business as
  Constellation Energy in 2022, serving roughly 10.7 million electric and
  gas customers across six utilities: ComEd, PECO, BGE, Pepco, Delmarva
  Power, and Atlantic City Electric. Its supply side depends on a small
  set of grid-equipment manufacturers and transmission contractors amid an
  industry-wide transformer shortage. Its customer base remains
  overwhelmingly the diversified retail territories it has always served,
  but a fast-growing, FERC-docketed pipeline of named hyperscale
  data-center agreements - roughly 18 GW committed as of late 2025 - is
  reshaping who drives Exelon's next wave of capital spending.
