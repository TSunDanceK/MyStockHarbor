summary: >
  UnitedHealth is a health insurer (UnitedHealthcare) and a health services
  group (Optum) that runs a pharmacy benefit manager, doctor practices and
  health technology. It makes almost nothing physical, so its dependencies
  are mostly contracts: with hospitals, physicians, drug makers, pharmacies
  and technology vendors on the supply side, and with the government above
  all on the customer side. Its 2025 10-K says premium revenue from the
  Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) was 44% of consolidated
  revenue. The filing describes its suppliers as groups of counterparties,
  so every supply-side entry below is a group rather than a named company,
  apart from Microsoft, which Optum announced a partnership with in March
  2026 on a claims platform.
