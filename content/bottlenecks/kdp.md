summary: >
  Keurig Dr Pepper makes and distributes Dr Pepper, 7UP, Snapple, Canada Dry,
  Mott's, and Keurig single-serve coffee brewers and K-Cup pods, and — since
  closing its roughly $18 billion acquisition of JDE Peet's in 2026 — also
  owns Peet's Coffee and Douwe Egberts, ahead of a planned future split into
  separate beverage and global coffee companies. Its supply chain runs
  through a small number of dominant aluminum can and packaging suppliers
  plus a fragmented global green coffee sourcing base, while its customer
  base is concentrated at the top around a handful of large US retailers,
  led by Walmart, a relationship the company itself discloses and quantifies
  in its SEC filings.
