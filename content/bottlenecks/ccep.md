summary: >
  Coca-Cola Europacific Partners is the world's largest independent Coca-Cola
  bottler, making, moving and selling Coca-Cola system drinks across Western
  Europe, Australia, the Pacific islands, Indonesia and Papua New Guinea. Its
  supply chain is dominated by one relationship above all others: The
  Coca-Cola Company, which supplies concentrate, licenses the brands under
  bottler's agreements, funds marketing, and holds a meaningful equity stake
  in CCEP itself, with can, bottle and sweetener makers filling out the rest
  of the cost base. On the customer side, revenue flows through a long tail
  of supermarkets, discounters, convenience stores and foodservice outlets
  across more than 30 countries, with a handful of large European and
  Australian grocery groups accounting for outsized, though not disclosed,
  shares of local sales.
