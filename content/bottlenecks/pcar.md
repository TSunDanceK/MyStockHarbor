summary: >
  PACCAR builds Kenworth and Peterbilt trucks in North America and DAF
  trucks in Europe, and its own 10-K states plainly that the company "is
  not dependent on any single customer for its sales" — trucks are sold
  almost entirely through independent dealer networks rather than to a
  concentrated set of large fleet buyers. The real dependency story runs
  through the supply chain instead: PACCAR builds only about 29-33% of
  its own engines in-house, relying on Cummins for the rest, along with
  transmission, axle, tire, and specialty-component suppliers it names
  directly in its own filings.
