summary: >
  PayPal Holdings is one of the world's largest digital payments platforms,
  operating the PayPal wallet, Venmo, the Braintree merchant-processing
  platform, and Xoom, and its business model is fundamentally a take-rate on
  payment volume rather than a physical-goods business. On the supply side,
  PayPal depends heavily on card network rails (Visa, Mastercard, American
  Express), a newly expanded Google Cloud infrastructure partnership, and
  specialized banking and fintech-infrastructure partners (The Bancorp,
  Synchrony, Plaid) it can't easily replace. On the customer side, PayPal's
  real base is tens of millions of diversified merchants and consumers with
  no material single-account concentration, so this page reframes that chart
  around its most significant named enterprise relationships — Uber,
  Shopify, and Airbnb — plus eBay, PayPal's former corporate parent and
  once-dominant customer, whose share has shrunk sharply since 2023.

  infrastructure it depends on to move money and run its platform — the
  chart below reflects that reality rather than physical component sourcing.
customersNote: >
  PayPal processes payments for tens of millions of merchants and hundreds

  PayPal's processing infrastructure, plus eBay, PayPal's former parent and
  one-time largest customer, whose share has shrunk sharply since eBay
  moved its primary processing to Adyen by mid-2023.
