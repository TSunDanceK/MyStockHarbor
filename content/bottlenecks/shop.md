summary: >
  Shopify (SHOP) is the e-commerce operating system behind millions of
  merchant storefronts, earning revenue mainly through subscription fees
  and a cut of merchant transaction volume rather than by selling a
  physical product. Its supply-side dependencies are concentrated in
  technology infrastructure rather than raw materials — Google Cloud runs
  the platform's compute backbone, Stripe and Adyen underpin its payments
  rails, and Flexport handles fulfillment and logistics. On the customer
  side no single merchant is material to revenue given Shopify's millions
  of small businesses, so the real concentration sits in a handful of
  enterprise relationships: an exclusive partnership with Global-e for
  cross-border commerce, the Amazon Buy with Prime integration, and
  marquee Shopify Plus/"Commerce Components" brands like Mattel, Coty,
  Steve Madden, Staples and Glossier that anchor its push upmarket.
