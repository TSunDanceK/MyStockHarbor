summary: >
  Fortinet designs custom security-processor ASICs and manufactures network
  security appliances, and its own 10-K discloses that roughly 87% of that
  hardware is made in Taiwan - a real, disclosed geographic and
  contract-manufacturer concentration. On the revenue side, Fortinet sells
  almost entirely through channel partners rather than directly to end
  customers, so its real concentration shows up among a small number of
  distributors rather than named end customers.
customersNote: >
  Fortinet sells almost entirely through channel partners rather than
  directly to end customers, so classic customer concentration doesn't
  apply in the usual sense. What Fortinet's 10-K does disclose is
  distributor concentration in its receivables: six distributors accounted
  for the large majority of net accounts receivable in recent years, with a
  single distributor alone representing about a third of it. This is a
  working-capital and channel-reach concentration risk, not a
  revenue-concentration risk - Fortinet's 10-K does not disclose any
  distributor or customer above 10% of total net revenue.
