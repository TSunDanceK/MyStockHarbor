summary: >
  Fastenal is an industrial distributor of fasteners, tools, safety supplies,
  and MRO products, selling through roughly 3,000 branches, on-site customer
  locations, and a fast-growing network of about 124,000 FASTVend industrial
  vending machines and bin-stock programs (together its "FMI" offering, 44.7%
  of 2025 sales). Its supply side leans heavily on manufacturers in China and
  Taiwan - global centers of fastener production - alongside a broad base of
  North American producers; its 10-K flags 2025 tariff increases and trade-policy
  risk as a direct cost pressure, and discloses that a single unnamed supplier
  crossed 5% of inventory purchases for the first time. On the demand side,
  Fastenal is the mirror image of a concentrated business: no single customer
  reached 5% of consolidated net sales in 2025, and revenue is instead spread
  across roughly three-quarters manufacturing accounts, with the rest split
  between non-residential construction and other end markets like government,
  reseller, and transportation customers.
