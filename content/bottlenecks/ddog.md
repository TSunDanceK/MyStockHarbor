  Datadog is a cloud-native SaaS platform for infrastructure monitoring,
  application performance monitoring, and log management, and its real
  "supply chain" is compute rather than components: its own 10-K
  discloses that it outsources substantially all of its infrastructure to
  third-party cloud hosts, led by AWS. Datadog has historically had no
  material customer concentration, but its newest filings disclose a
  fresh wrinkle - a fast-growing "AI-native" customer cohort, including a
  single largest customer several analysts have identified as OpenAI, now
  drives a meaningful share of its growth.
