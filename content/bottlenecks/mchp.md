summary: >
  Microchip Technology designs and manufactures microcontrollers, analog and
  mixed-signal chips, FPGAs, and memory products used across industrial,
  automotive, aerospace & defense, communications, computing, and consumer
  electronics. Like Analog Devices, it is a hybrid integrated device
  manufacturer: it still runs its own wafer fabs in Gresham, Oregon (Fab 4)
  and Colorado Springs, Colorado (Fab 5), after closing its older Tempe,
  Arizona fab (Fab 2) in 2025. But a majority of its output, about 64% of
  fiscal 2025 net sales, comes from products built at outside wafer
  foundries, and roughly a third of its assembly and test work is
  outsourced to third-party contractors. On the revenue side, Microchip
  serves an extremely broad base of roughly 109,000 customers, but its
  10-K discloses that one distributor, Arrow Electronics, crosses the
  company's 10%-of-net-sales disclosure threshold, and its end markets have
  shifted sharply toward aerospace & defense in the past year.
