summary: >
  Gilead Sciences is a biopharmaceutical giant built on HIV franchises like
  Biktarvy and Descovy, alongside Veklury (remdesivir), oncology drug
  Trodelvy, and Kite's CAR-T cell therapies Yescarta and Tecartus. On the
  supply side, Gilead deliberately spreads manufacturing across multiple
  contract manufacturers and backup sites rather than leaning on one or two
  vendors, though a handful of named partners like Korea's Yuhan and Italy's
  Flamma stand out. On the customer side, the real concentration risk is
  distribution: by Gilead's own SEC filings, a small handful of US wholesale
  drug distributors move the vast majority of its US product volume.
