summary: >
  Vertex dominates the cystic fibrosis drug market with Trikafta/Kaftrio and
  its predecessors, and has expanded into gene therapy with Casgevy, its
  CRISPR-based treatment developed with CRISPR Therapeutics. Its
  manufacturing dependencies are concentrated in a small number of named
  cell-therapy contract manufacturers behind Casgevy, while its revenue
  flows through a short, genuinely concentrated list of pharmacy-benefit
  wholesalers and specialty pharmacies that Vertex itself names as its
  authorized distributors.

  gene-therapy franchise, where contract manufacturers are disclosed
