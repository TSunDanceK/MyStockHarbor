summary: >
  Regeneron discovers and manufactures its own drugs, but two of its
  biggest products are actually co-developed and co-commercialized with
  larger partners: Sanofi (Dupixent, Kevzara, Praluent) and Bayer (EYLEA
  outside the US). Those partnerships cut both ways in this file — Sanofi
  and Bayer are supply-side collaborators that fund and share development
  costs, but their profit-share payments back to Regeneron are also so
  large that they function as Regeneron's biggest "customers" in revenue
  terms: Sanofi alone accounted for 41% of Regeneron's total 2025 revenue.
  On the pure product side, two pharmaceutical wholesalers — Besse Medical
  and McKesson — together made up roughly 77% of Regeneron's gross
  product revenue in recent years, a separate and equally real
  concentration risk measured on a different base.
