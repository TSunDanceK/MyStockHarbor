summary: >
  Monster Beverage designs and markets energy drinks and other beverages but
  owns almost none of the physical infrastructure that makes and moves them
  - it depends on a consolidated aluminum-can industry, sole-source flavor
  suppliers, and an increasingly concentrated group of third-party co-packers
  to produce its drinks. On the demand side, its defining dependency is the
  2015 strategic alliance with The Coca-Cola Company: Monster's own filings
  say essentially all U.S. territories and substantially all international
  territories have been transitioned to Coca-Cola's bottler network, making
  a handful of Coca-Cola-system distributors the channel for the large
  majority of its global volume.
