summary: >
  Warner Bros. Discovery owns HBO Max, CNN, TNT, Discovery, and the Warner
  Bros. film and TV studio, but its corporate future is unusually uncertain:
  a planned 2025 split into separate streaming/studio and linear-networks
  companies was scrapped in February 2026 in favor of a $110.9 billion,
  all-cash sale of the entire company to Paramount Skydance, which
  shareholders approved in April 2026. That deal has since been paused by a
  federal court's July 2026 temporary restraining order following antitrust
  lawsuits from a coalition of state attorneys general and the Writers
  Guild of America, with the companies agreeing not to close before June
  2027 (or five days after the litigation ends) — so WBD remains, for now,
  a standalone public company with real operating dependencies of its own.
  On the supply side it leans heavily on AWS for cloud infrastructure and
  on costly sports-content licensing; on the customer side its revenue runs
  through a shifting mix of cable, satellite, and streaming distributors
  rather than any single dominant buyer.
