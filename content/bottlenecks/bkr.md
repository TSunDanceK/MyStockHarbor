summary: >
  Baker Hughes supplies oilfield services, equipment, and increasingly LNG
  and energy-transition technology, split between its Oilfield Services &
  Equipment (OFSE) segment and its faster-growing Industrial & Energy
  Technology (IET) segment. Its supply chain depends heavily on former
  parent GE's two spinoffs, GE Vernova and GE Aerospace, for turbines and
  components under long-term supply agreements, plus specialty-alloy
  producers for the nickel, titanium, tungsten carbide, and beryllium its
  equipment requires. On the customer side, Baker Hughes' 10-K discloses
  no single buyer above 10% of total revenue, but its order backlog is
  genuinely concentrated: $32.4 billion of its $35.9 billion total backlog
  sits in IET, driven by a short list of national oil companies, oil
  majors, and LNG megaproject sponsors.
