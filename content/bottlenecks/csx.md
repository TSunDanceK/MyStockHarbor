summary: >
  CSX operates roughly 19,500 route miles of freight rail across the eastern
  United States, and its dependency story runs in two very different
  directions. On the supply side, it leans on a handful of capital-goods
  makers that effectively have no substitutes — locomotive builders, the one
  surviving North American rail-steel mill, and specialized tie and
  trackwork suppliers. On the revenue side, CSX doesn't disclose individual
  shipper names or customer concentration (a normal feature of Class I
  railroad reporting), so the more honest picture of its dependency is the
  commodity mix it actually reports: chemicals, intermodal, and coal
  together drive nearly half of revenue.
