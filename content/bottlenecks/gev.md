summary: >
  GE Vernova, spun off from General Electric in 2024, designs and manufactures
  gas turbines, wind turbines, grid/electrification equipment, and nuclear
  technology including the BWRX-300 small modular reactor. AI and data-center
  driven electricity demand has pushed its gas turbine and grid order backlogs
  to record, multi-year-sold-out levels. Its supply chain runs through a small
  set of genuinely concentrated dependencies -- turbine-grade forgings and
  castings, semiconductor/power-electronics content, and rare-earth and other
  critical minerals largely tied to Chinese processing -- each flagged in its
  own 10-K as a limited- or sole-source risk. Its customer base, by contrast,
  is broadly diversified across thousands of utilities and governments
  worldwide, though a handful of high-profile relationships now account for
  an outsized share of its recent order growth.
customersNote: >
