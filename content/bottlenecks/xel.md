summary: >
  Xcel Energy is a regulated electric and natural gas holding company serving
  roughly 3.8 million electricity and 2.2 million natural gas customers across
  Minnesota, Colorado, Texas/New Mexico, Wisconsin/Michigan and the Dakotas
  through four operating utilities. Its supply chain runs through nuclear fuel
  fabricators for its Monticello and Prairie Island plants, wind and gas
  turbine manufacturers racing to keep pace with a multi-year equipment
  backlog, and the remaining natural gas and Powder River Basin coal that
  fuel its thermal fleet. On the demand side, Xcel has moved beyond its
  traditional diversified retail base to disclose a fast-growing, named
  hyperscale data-center pipeline (Microsoft, Meta, Google/Alphabet, QTS)
  that management says now drives a significant share of the company's new
  load growth.
