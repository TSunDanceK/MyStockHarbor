summary: >
  PDD Holdings is the asset-light operator behind Pinduoduo (China's
  group-buying marketplace) and Temu (its fast-growing international
  discount marketplace) - it owns no factories and manufactures nothing
  itself. Its real dependencies sit in cloud infrastructure, cross-border
  logistics, and app-store distribution, several of which are concentrated
  around a small number of named partners. On the revenue side, PDD's
  business is fundamentally diversified across hundreds of millions of
  individual consumers and small merchants, with no disclosed concentrated
  business customer at all.
customersNote: >
  PDD does not have a concentrated business customer in the way a hardware
  or cloud company does - its revenue comes from hundreds of millions of
  individual consumers and a large, fragmented base of small-to-mid
  merchants across Pinduoduo (China) and Temu (international) paying
  transaction and marketing fees. PDD also does not publicly disclose a
  revenue split between its two platforms. The chart below is a rough,
  illustrative split based on general industry and analyst commentary about
  the relative scale of each platform, not a company-disclosed figure -
  treat it as directional only, not precise.
