summary: >
  Meta's advertising revenue comes from millions of advertisers with no
  single advertiser representing a materially concentrated share - a real
  strength, not a bottleneck. Where Meta does depend heavily on outside
  partners is its AI infrastructure: it's one of Nvidia's largest GPU
  customers and depends on TSMC and Broadcom to build the custom silicon
  behind its in-house MTIA AI chips.
