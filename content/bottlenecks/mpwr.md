  Monolithic Power Systems is a fabless analog and power-management

  automotive, storage, and industrial applications. As a fabless company
  it depends entirely on third-party foundries and outsourced packaging
  partners across China, Taiwan, South Korea, and Singapore, and its
  revenue runs through a small number of large distributors - the top
  three accounted for 54% of 2025 revenue - a concentration credible
  reporting has tied to surging NVIDIA/AI-datacenter demand for its power
  delivery chips.
customersNote: >

  disclose which end customers sit behind that distributor revenue, but
  industry reporting (including SemiAnalysis) has documented MPWR

  platforms, and MPWR's own results show its Enterprise Data segment
  reaching roughly a third of quarterly revenue in early 2026 - the
  likely driver behind the top distributor's outsized share.
