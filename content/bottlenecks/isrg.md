summary: >
  Intuitive Surgical designs, builds and sells the da Vinci and Ion robotic
  surgical platforms, along with the recurring instruments, software and
  service revenue that now make up the bulk of its business. On the supply
  side it leans on a small number of identifiable technology partners — a
  Swiss precision-motor maker, a Swedish simulation-software provider and
  NVIDIA's newest AI computing platform — layered on top of a broader,
  undisclosed base of sole- and single-source component vendors flagged in
  its own SEC filings. On the customer side, Intuitive sells to thousands of
  individual hospitals worldwide, so no single buyer is a material share of
  revenue; the more meaningful concentration story is the handful of very
  large U.S. hospital systems that negotiate system-wide, sometimes
  effectively sole-source, purchasing agreements, set against the roughly
  one-third of revenue now generated outside the United States.
supplyChainNote: >
  Intuitive does not publicly name most of its component suppliers. Its own
  SEC filings describe reliance on numerous "sole-sourced" and
  "single-sourced" vendors — for precision-machined parts, electronics and
  other components — purchased under standard purchase orders rather than
  long-term supply contracts. The list below combines the few technology
  partnerships that are publicly identifiable with that broader, undisclosed
  sole-source vendor base, to give a realistic rather than padded picture.
customersNote: >
  Intuitive sells its systems and instruments to thousands of individual
  hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers and integrated delivery networks
  around the world, and it does not disclose — nor does the underlying
  business realistically have — any single hospital or health system that
  represents a material share of revenue. The more meaningful concentration
  lies in a handful of very large U.S. hospital systems that negotiate
  enterprise-wide, sometimes effectively sole-source, purchasing agreements
  across dozens of facilities at once, set against the roughly one-third of
  total revenue Intuitive now generates outside the United States.
