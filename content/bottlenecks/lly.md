summary: >
  Lilly makes medicines for diabetes, obesity, cancer, immunology and
  Alzheimer's disease, and its tirzepatide drugs Mounjaro and Zepbound made
  56% of its $65.2 billion revenue in 2025. Lilly makes much of its own
  supply and is building more plants, but its 10-K says it also uses third
  parties for some active ingredient manufacturing, filling and finishing,
  and device production, and that certain materials come from a single
  source. On the customer side, three US drug wholesalers, McKesson,
  Cencora and Cardinal Health, each made between 16% and 24% of 2025
  revenue.
