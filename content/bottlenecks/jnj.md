summary: >
  Johnson & Johnson runs two businesses, Innovative Medicine (more than $60
  billion of 2025 sales) and MedTech (nearly $34 billion), and reported $94.2
  billion of sales in fiscal 2025. Its 10-K says raw materials are generally
  readily available from multiple sources, but also that it relies on third
  parties to manufacture and supply certain products. Several of its biggest
  medicines depend on partners: Genmab licensed the antibody behind
  Darzalex to J&J, Legend Biotech co-develops Carvykti, and Protagonist
  discovered icotrokinra with J&J. On the customer side, this map names no
  customer or wholesaler and shows no filed customer percentage, so it groups
  customers by channel and region instead.
