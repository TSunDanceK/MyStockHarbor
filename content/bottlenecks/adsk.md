summary: >
  Autodesk sells subscription design and engineering software - AutoCAD,
  Revit, Maya, Fusion, and Construction Cloud - to architecture,
  engineering, construction, manufacturing, and media customers. As a
  software company its "supply chain" is cloud infrastructure and AI
  compute rather than physical manufacturing. Its real, disclosed
  concentration sits on the customer side: Autodesk's own 10-K names TD
  Synnex as a distributor that alone accounted for 39% of revenue in
  fiscal 2024, a dependency it has been actively unwinding as it shifts
  customers toward direct billing - down to 14% by fiscal 2026.
customersNote: >
  Autodesk's 10-K discloses no end-customer concentration - no single
  buyer approaches a meaningful share of revenue - but it does disclose a
  real, named, and unusually large distributor dependency: TD Synnex
  Corporation alone accounted for 39%, 33%, and 14% of Autodesk's net
  revenue in fiscal years 2024, 2025, and 2026 respectively, as Autodesk
  actively shifts customers to direct transacting. The chart below is
  built around this real distributor and channel-partner concentration
  rather than end-customer names, which Autodesk doesn't disclose.
