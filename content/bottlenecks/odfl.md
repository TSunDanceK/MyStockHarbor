summary: >
  Old Dominion is a non-union, asset-based less-than-truckload carrier
  running 260 service centers (at December 31, 2025) across North America. Its supply
  side leans on a small handful of tractor and trailer manufacturers - it
  doesn't build its own equipment - plus the diesel fuel market, one of
  its largest operating costs. On the customer side, Old Dominion's own
  10-K discloses real, if modest, concentration: its largest customer
  alone was about 4% of 2025 revenue, and its top 20 accounts together
  made up about 23% - concentration Old Dominion tracks but doesn't name
  individual customers for.
