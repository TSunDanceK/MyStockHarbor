summary: >
  NXP Semiconductors is a Netherlands-headquartered chipmaker built around
  automotive, industrial, and IoT applications, using a "hybrid
  manufacturing model" that mixes owned fabs with joint-venture foundries
  run alongside TSMC. Its supply chain is concentrated around a small
  number of very hard-to-replace partners - the TSMC joint ventures, Arm's
  processor architecture, and the Synopsys/Cadence design-software duopoly
  - while its revenue is the opposite: automotive, industrial, and mobile
  customers are spread across thousands of accounts, with distributor
  Avnet as the one genuinely concentrated relationship NXP discloses.
customersNote: >
  NXP's 10-K states that no direct customer accounted for more than 10% of
  its revenue in 2025 or 2024, reflecting a broad base of automotive,
  industrial, and mobile OEM customers. The one real concentration NXP
  does disclose is channel-based: Avnet, its largest distributor,
  represented 23% of 2025 revenue, with no other distributor above 10%.
  This page reflects that disclosed distributor concentration rather than
  forcing a misleading chart of named OEM customers NXP doesn't break out
  individually.
