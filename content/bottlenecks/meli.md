summary: >
  MercadoLibre runs Latin America's largest online marketplace and, through
  Mercado Pago, one of the region's largest digital payments and lending
  businesses. On the supply side it leans on a small number of hard-to-swap
  partners — Amazon Web Services for its core cloud infrastructure, Visa
  and Mastercard for card payment rails, and Brazil's postal service for
  last-mile delivery in a market it can't fully cover with its own Mercado
  Envios network. On the revenue side there is no concentrated customer to
  point to — MercadoLibre sells to tens of millions of individual buyers
  and small merchants — so the real concentration risk it discloses is
  geographic: a majority of revenue still comes from a single country,
  Brazil.

  discloses: how dependent its revenue is on performance in a single
  country, Brazil, versus its other markets.
