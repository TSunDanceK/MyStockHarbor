summary: >
  Strategy Inc (formerly MicroStrategy) has transformed from a mid-size enterprise
  business-intelligence software vendor into primarily a Bitcoin treasury company,
  holding hundreds of thousands of bitcoin funded by convertible notes, preferred
  stock, and at-the-market equity sales, alongside a much smaller legacy "Strategy
  Enterprise Analytics" software unit. Its operational bottlenecks are unusual for
  a public company: rather than physical suppliers, it depends on a small set of
  regulated digital-asset custodians that safeguard its bitcoin and a syndicate of
  Wall Street banks that keep its capital-raising machine running. Because its
  software customer base is broadly diversified with no disclosed material
  customer, the "customer" side of Strategy's story is really the capital markets
  and shareholder base that absorbs its stock and debt to keep funding bitcoin
  purchases.
supplyChainNote: >
  Strategy does not have a conventional physical supply chain. What actually gates
  its operations is (1) the regulated custodians holding its bitcoin, which the
  company names explicitly in its SEC filings with exact holding splits, (2) the
  bank syndicate that underwrites its convertible-note, preferred-stock, and
  at-the-market equity programs, which fund essentially all of its bitcoin
  purchases, and (3) the cloud infrastructure providers underpinning its smaller
  legacy analytics software business. This chart reframes "supply chain" around
  those three dependencies rather than forcing a traditional vendor list.
customersNote: >
  Strategy's legacy enterprise analytics software business serves thousands of
  diversified corporate and government customers, and the company does not
  disclose any single customer as material to revenue. The more meaningful
  concentration story today is on the other side of the balance sheet: who
  actually buys and holds Strategy's stock and capital-raising instruments,
  since that investor base is what funds continued bitcoin accumulation. This
  chart shows Strategy's disclosed shareholder base instead of a traditional
  customer list.
