summary: >
  Intuit builds and sells TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma, and Mailchimp
  to tens of millions of consumers, small businesses, and accountants, so
  it has no genuine customer-concentration story — no single client moves
  the needle. Its real dependency risk sits upstream, in a small set of
  technology and financial-infrastructure providers it leans on to run
  those products: public cloud platforms, credit-bureau data for Credit
  Karma, bank partners that hold the actual deposits behind its banking
  features, and the e-file "pipes" into the IRS and state tax agencies
  that TurboTax cannot function without. On the revenue side, this page
  instead shows how Intuit's own revenue concentrates across its reported
  product segments.
