The short version

Amazon slipped about 2% under its 200-day average two days before its Q2 report, after Alphabet's bigger capex plan raised worries about AI spending. The report on 30 Jul showed EPS up sharply on a year earlier, and the stock has since moved back above the line.

Why the 200-day mattered here

The 200-day average is a long-term trend line many chart readers watch. A close back above it may suggest the longer uptrend held; a run of closes below it might suggest a deeper pullback. It's one signal, not a forecast.
